# Requirements starter — Minnesota

Built from the text of Minn. Stat. § 13.32 as published by the Office of the Revisor of Statutes (subdivisions 13 and 14 added by 2022 Minn. Laws ch. 69; the Revisor's section history shows no amendments to subdivisions 13–15 through the 2025 statutes), and the FERPA school-official conditions at 34 C.F.R. § 99.31(a)(1)(i)(B). **This is a starting point, not legal advice.** Verify against the current statute at revisor.mn.gov and with counsel. Paraphrases below are short; the statute's exact words govern.

Note that subd. 13 applies to "technology providers" as defined in subd. 1(g). Whether a given vendor meets that definition is itself a question for counsel.

## Contract and provider obligations — Minn. Stat. § 13.32, subd. 13

| ID | Requirement (paraphrase — check statute text) | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| MN-1 | Provider is subject to § 13.05, subd. 11 (contractors performing government functions are bound by the Data Practices Act) | subd. 13(a) |
| MN-2 | Educational data created, received, maintained, or disseminated under the contract are not the provider's property | subd. 13(b) |
| MN-3 | After discovering a breach, provider discloses to the district all information needed to meet § 13.055 | subd. 13(c) |
| MN-4 | Unless renewal is reasonably anticipated, within 90 days of contract expiration, provider destroys or returns all educational data | subd. 13(d) |
| MN-5 | Provider does not sell, share, or disseminate educational data except as the section allows or via a valid assignment/delegation; assignees bound by the same terms | subd. 13(e) |
| MN-6 | No commercial use of educational data, including marketing or advertising to students or parents (providing the contracted services is not commercial use; de-identified aggregate use to improve, maintain, develop, support, or diagnose the provider's own service is not prohibited) | subd. 13(f) |
| MN-7 | Contract requires security safeguards, including that provider employees and contractors access data only if authorized, and only when necessary for their official duties | subd. 13(g) |

## District obligations (not contract terms, but the contract should make them possible)
