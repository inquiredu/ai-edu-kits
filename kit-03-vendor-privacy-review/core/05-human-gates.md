# Human gates

## Gate 1 — Requirements and documents
**Owner:** `{{REQUIREMENTS_OWNER}}`.
Is the requirements file current and approved? Are all incorporated documents included, with versions and capture dates? Have you checked the contract's confidentiality terms before pasting?

## Gate 2 — Decide
**Owners:** `{{COUNSEL}}` and `{{SIGNER}}`.
Review the audited gap map and the counsel list. Decide: sign, negotiate (send vendor questions or the district exhibit), or decline. Record the decision and the versions reviewed. The kit's output is working material for that decision, not a record of compliance.

## After signing
Add the vendor to your MN-8 annual notice list if it is a curriculum, testing, or assessment provider, and calendar the MN-4 end-of-contract data return or destruction check.
