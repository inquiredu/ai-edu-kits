### Doc A: BrightPath Data Processing Addendum, v2026-04
1.1 Precedence. In the event of a conflict between this Addendum and the BrightPath Terms of Service, this Addendum controls.
2.1 Ownership. Customer retains all right, title, and interest in Student Data. BrightPath acquires no ownership interest in Student Data.
3.1 Access. BrightPath limits access to Student Data to personnel who require access to provide the Services.
4.1 Use. BrightPath uses Student Data solely to provide the Services and will not use Student Data for targeted advertising.
4.3 De-identified Data. BrightPath may use de-identified data for product improvement and may share de-identified data with research partners.
6.2 Security Incidents. BrightPath will notify Customer of a confirmed security incident affecting Student Data within a commercially reasonable time and will provide information reasonably requested by Customer.
7.2 Retention. Upon termination, BrightPath will delete Student Data within 180 days, unless Customer requests return in writing.
