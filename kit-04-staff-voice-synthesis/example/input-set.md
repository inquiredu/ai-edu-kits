## Context
Group: Middle school instructional and support staff, Lakeview Public Schools (fictional)
Question asked: "What do you need to feel confident about students using AI in your classroom this year?"
Collection method: Anonymous form, optional role field
Date collected: [example]
Total responses: 16
Role attribution: agreed in advance — the form stated role labels would appear in the summary, including for roles with one respondent
Responses withheld at Gate 1: 0

## Responses
R01 | ELA | I need a clear way to tell students when AI is okay on an assignment and when it isn't. Right now every teacher does it differently and kids are confused.
R02 | Science | Honestly, time. I've opened the tool twice. I'd need an hour with someone showing me what it's good for in a lab unit.
R03 | ELA | My worry is the writing. If they can get a paragraph instantly, when do they learn to wrestle with a sentence? I want protected space for that.
R04 | Math | Which tools are actually approved? I don't want to recommend something and find out later it wasn't allowed. Also a simple label for assignments.
R05 | Social Studies | Parents are going to ask me. I'd like a letter or talking points from the district so I'm not making it up.
R06 | Special Education | For some of my students, AI read-aloud and summarizing is an accommodation. For others I worry it hides what they can't do yet, and then we miss it. I need help telling the difference.
R07 | Science | Consistency across the team. If 7th grade science says one thing and 7th ELA another, kids play us against each other.
R08 | Elective | Time to play with it myself without it being evaluated.
R09 | Math | Problem solving is the point in my class. I'd want assignments where the struggle stays with the student.
R10 | ELA | A shared scale we all use, posted in the room, so the conversation is about the level, not about whether they cheated.
R11 | Counselor | Families have really different comfort levels. Some will want to opt out. What do we say?
R12 | Social Studies | A cohort or a PLC slot to try things with colleagues. I learn by seeing someone else's lesson.
R13 | Science | Clear expectations on every assignment. That's it.
R14 | ELA | I'm less worried about cheating than about kids not trusting their own ideas. Where does their voice go?
R15 | Math | I don't think it belongs in my classroom this year. I'd rather keep it out until we know more, and I'd like that to be okay.
R16 | Elective | Know what data the tools keep about students. Kids ask me and I don't have an answer.
