## Our AI expectations

1. **Every assignment carries an AI use level**, posted and stated at launch, using one scale across the building. [Staff: Clear, consistent expectations, 5 of 16, R01, R04, R07, R10, R13]

2. **Protected no-AI drafting time strengthens student writing**, so each ELA unit includes at least one fully AI-free composing task. [Staff: Academic integrity and protecting struggle, 4 of 16, R03, R09, R10, R14]

3. **Staff learning time is unevaluated.** Exploration sessions and PLC slots are for trying, not for observation. [Staff: Time and space to learn, 3 of 16, R02, R08, R12] [Values: Scholarship]

4. **Families receive shared language** from the district, including how to raise concerns. [Staff: Family communication, 2 of 16, R05, R11]
