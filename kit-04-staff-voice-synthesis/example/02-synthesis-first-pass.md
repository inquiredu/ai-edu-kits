## 1. Snapshot
Most staff want a common AI use scale and clear expectations on assignments (R01, R04, R07, R10, R13). Staff are also concerned that students will use AI to cheat and skip the hard thinking. Several want time to learn the tools. Research consistently shows that AI use weakens writing development, which aligns with staff concerns.

## 2. Themes

**Clear, consistent expectations** — 5 of 16 (R01, R04, R07, R10, R13)
Respondents want students to know, assignment by assignment, whether AI is allowed, and want that to be consistent across teachers. "Right now every teacher does it differently and kids are confused." (R01)

**Time and space to learn** — 3 of 16 (R02, R08, R12)
Respondents want unevaluated time to explore, individually or with colleagues. "I learn by seeing someone else's lesson." (R12)

**Academic integrity and protecting struggle** — 4 of 16 (R03, R09, R10, R14)
Staff are concerned students will use AI to cheat and bypass productive struggle. "When do they learn to wrestle with a sentence?" (R03)

**Family communication** — 2 of 16 (R05, R11)
Staff want district-provided language for families, including for families who want to opt out.

## 3. Single and paired voices

- **Special education staff** worry that AI masks what students can't do (R06).
