# Human gates

Three decisions in this process belong to people. The AI roles stop and wait at each one.

## Gate 1 — Before any AI sees the input
**Owner:** whoever holds the raw data.

- Which lane am I in? (`core/01-guardrails.md`)
- Names, identifying details, and small role groups handled?
- Responses withheld, and how many, recorded?
- Input shaped to `core/02-inputs-template.md`?

After the Preparer runs, review its flags and resolve each one yourself. Proceed only when you would be comfortable with every respondent seeing exactly what the tool saw.

## Gate 2 — After the synthesis, before drafting
**Owner:** the person running the process, ideally with one colleague who was not.

- Would respondents recognize themselves? Read the single-voice and dissent sections first.
- Does any theme feel bigger or smaller than it was in the room?
- Is anything important missing that you know was said in person but not written? (If so, it cannot be added as data — note it separately as facilitator observation.)

Optional and worth it: share the theme list with two or three respondents and ask, "Did we get this right?"

## Closing the loop

The step most processes skip: telling staff what you heard and what you did with it — including what you chose not to act on and why. A synthesis nobody sees back teaches people that giving input goes nowhere. Share the theme list and your decisions within a timeframe you name in advance.
