# Rubric

Use this at Gate 3, or any time you want to judge a run. The Auditor checks the first six rows mechanically; the last two need a person.

| Criterion | Strong | Needs revision |
|---|---|---|
| **Fidelity** | Every claim traces to response IDs; quotes are verbatim | Paraphrase drifts from what was written; quotes altered or unattributed |
| **Attribution level** | Counts stated; "most/many/staff" used only when the numbers support it; single voices labeled as single | A few voices generalized to a group; role groups characterized from one member |
| **Dissent kept** | Minority and opposing views have their own section, stated fairly and in their own terms | Dissent dropped, softened, or framed as an obstacle |
| **Silence named** | Topics relevant to `{{PURPOSE}}` that nobody raised are listed as open questions | Absence treated as agreement or ignored |
| **Evidence lanes** | Staff voice and research kept separate; no citation outside the evidence pack | "Research shows…" with no vetted source; staff comments presented as evidence of effectiveness |
| **Privacy** | No names, no role label under `{{MIN_GROUP_SIZE}}` without recorded consent, no identifying detail | Anything that lets a colleague say "that's obviously ___" |
| **Tone** *(person)* | Challenge, not threat; staff treated as professionals with reasons | Alarm, deficit framing, "resistance" language |
| **Decision-ready** *(person)* | `{{DECISION_OWNER}}` can see what is settled, what is contested, and what they must decide | Reads as a report to file, not a basis for action |

A useful last test: hand the synthesis to someone who responded. If they can find their comment's meaning intact — even when it was a minority view — the run did its job.
