# Human gates

## Gate 1 — Assemble the source
**Owner:** whoever is producing the translation.
Is the policy text the adopted version, verbatim, with numbering intact? Are referenced guidelines current and dated?

## Gate 2 — Rule on ambiguity
**Owner:** `{{POLICY_OWNER}}`.
The Reader's clause map lists every silent or ambiguous point. For each, the owner records one of:
- **Ruling:** "The district reads §X to mean ___." (becomes an [Interpretation] tag)
- **Defer:** "This goes to the next policy review." (the translation names it as an open question)
- **Guidance:** "Our guidelines address this in ___." (becomes a [Guidance] tag)

Record rulings in a short list. Only recorded rulings may appear in the translation.

## Gate 3 — Approve for distribution
**Owner:** `{{POLICY_OWNER}}`.
Review the audit, resolve every HIGH flag, and approve. Date-stamp the translation and note which policy version it reflects, so it can be retired when the policy changes.

## After distribution
Collect the questions the translation didn't answer. They are your agenda for the next policy review — and good input for Kit 04.
