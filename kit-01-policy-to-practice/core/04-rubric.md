# Rubric

| Criterion | Strong | Needs revision |
|---|---|---|
| **Fidelity** | Every statement traces to a section; paraphrase carries the same obligation | Obligation added, dropped, or shifted |
| **Modal precision** | must/may/should preserved exactly | "may" rendered as "must," or the reverse |
| **Tag honesty** | Interpretation is labeled as interpretation, with the ruling owner | District reading presented as policy text |
| **Ambiguity surfaced** | Open questions are named and routed | Ambiguity silently resolved |
| **Guidance separated** | Guideline content labeled as changeable guidance | Guideline treated as board policy |
| **Routing** | Reader knows who to ask for what | Questions left with nowhere to go |
| **Tone** *(person)* | Peer-to-peer; respects site expertise | Pedantic, compliance-threat, or alarmed |
| **Usable** *(person)* | A principal could explain it to staff tomorrow | Accurate but unusable |
