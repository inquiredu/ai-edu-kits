# Inputs template

```
# Policy input set

## Context
Policy: {{POLICY_NUMBER}} — {{POLICY_TITLE}}
Adopted: [date]    Source: [where the official text lives]
Audiences for translation: {{AUDIENCES}}
Interpretation owner: {{POLICY_OWNER}}

## Policy text (verbatim, with section numbering preserved)
[paste]

## Referenced documents (optional)
G1 | [guideline or procedure name, version/date] | [relevant text]
...

## Known questions from the field (optional)
Q1 | [question as asked by staff or administrators, de-identified]
...
```

Preserve section numbering exactly — every tag depends on it. If the policy references a statute or another policy, include the referenced text or note that it was not provided; the roles will not fill it in from memory. "Known questions from the field" are gold: they show where confusion already lives, and the Reader checks whether the policy answers each one.
