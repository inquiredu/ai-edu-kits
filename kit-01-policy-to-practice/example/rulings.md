# Gate 2 rulings — SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE (Superintendent)

R-1 | §V with §II.A and §III.A | Ruling: The district reads §V together with §II and §III.A. Confidential data may be entered only into approved tools covered by a district data agreement, and only as the Technology Tools Guidelines allow. It may not be entered into any other tool.
R-2 | §IV.B | Defer: "Any technology tool" read literally would include district login systems. Refer to the next policy review. Until then, the translation names this as an open question.
