Adopted: [example]
Audiences for translation: building administrators (guide); instructional staff (one page)
Interpretation owner: Superintendent

## Policy text
I. PURPOSE
The district supports staff and student use of technology tools, including artificial intelligence tools, to advance learning.

II. DEFINITIONS
A. "Approved tool" means a technology tool reviewed and listed by the district technology office.
B. "Confidential data" means data classified as private or confidential under state or federal law.

III. STAFF USE
A. Staff may use approved tools for instruction and district operations.
B. Staff should model responsible use of technology tools for students.
C. Staff are encouraged to explore emerging tools for their own professional learning.

IV. STUDENT USE
A. Students may use approved tools as directed by the teacher.
B. Students must not enter personal information into any technology tool.

V. DATA
Staff must not input confidential data into technology tools.

VI. IMPLEMENTATION
The superintendent shall establish guidelines to implement this policy.
