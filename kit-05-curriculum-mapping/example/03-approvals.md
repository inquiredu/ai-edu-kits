# Gate 2 approvals — format example only

The worked example review CUTs more than half the subject's moments. That stop must be resolved by a person before Gate 2; this blank format does not override it and grants no approval.

| Subject | Code / moment ID | Proposal source (Mapper / reviewer-proposed / No AI) | Decision (approve / revise / cut) | Approved moment text, including exact standard | Revision notes | Curriculum lead | Subject teacher | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Record every proposal, including No AI recommendations and reviewer-proposed moments. A revise decision is not an approval until the two owners approve the revised wording. Only approved moment text and its relevant review and revision notes go to the Moment writer. An approved No AI recommendation is retained as a curriculum decision; it does not request an AI moment card.
