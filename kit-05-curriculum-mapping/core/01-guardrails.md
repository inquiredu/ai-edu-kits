# Guardrails

## Tool lane
Standards, scales, and curriculum documents are not sensitive. This kit uses no student data, and no student work should be pasted into it. If you want to test a moment with sample student work, write the sample yourself.

## Moments must stay inside what the district allows
- Only tools on `{{APPROVED_TOOLS}}`, and only in ways their terms allow for `{{GRADE}}` students. Many AI tools set minimum ages in their terms; check each tool's current terms rather than assuming.
- No moment asks students to enter personal information, other students' work, photos, or voices into any tool.
- Consumer harms — companion chatbots, deepfakes, voice cloning, scams — are **discussed and analyzed, never produced or demonstrated** by students. A teacher may prepare a clearly labeled example for analysis.

## Evidence lane
The mapper may describe what a moment is designed to do. It may not claim a moment improves learning, engagement, or outcomes unless a source in the evidence pack supports it. Otherwise the claim becomes a research question. The rubric's lenses name researchers as lenses for review, not as proof that any particular moment works.

## What the AI roles may and may not do

| May | May not |
|---|---|
| Propose moments, including "none" | Claim a moment works |
| Name the thinking a standard requires | Rewrite or paraphrase the standard as if official |
| Flag risks and suggest fixes | Approve moments for classroom use |
| Draft teacher cards for approved moments | Recommend unapproved tools |
