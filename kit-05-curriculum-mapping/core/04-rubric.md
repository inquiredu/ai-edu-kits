# Rubric — the pedagogy reviewer's lenses

Each lens is a question to ask of every proposed moment. The named researchers are the lens, not a claim about any specific moment.

| # | Lens | The question | HIGH flag when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Safety as precondition** (Hammond) | Could this moment make a student feel exposed, judged, or unsafe? A brain prioritizing safety deprioritizes academic learning. | Public comparison with AI output, grading against AI, or content that could shame or distress |
| 2 | **The thinking stays with the student** (Bjork, desirable difficulties; Kapur, productive failure) | Does the student do the cognitive work the standard's verb names, or does AI? | AI performs the standard's verb — finds the evidence, makes the argument, draws the inference |
| 3 | **Access** (CAST, Universal Design for Learning) | Can every student get in? Are there multiple means of engagement, representation, and expression? Does it assume home access or accounts? | Only one path in; requires home access or a personal account |
| 4 | **Developmental fit** | Does it fit grade `{{GRADE}}` and the progression? | Beyond or beneath the progression; mismatched to age |
| 5 | **Privacy and tools** | Approved tools only, within their age terms? No personal info, others' work, images, or voices? | Unapproved tool; student data or work of others entered; images or voices of real people |
| 6 | **Harm** | Are consumer risks discussed rather than produced? | Students create deepfakes, clone voices, simulate scams, or roleplay companions |
| 7 | **Equity of impact** | Who benefits most, and who could be left behind or disadvantaged? | Advantage depends on prior tech fluency or home resources, with no support |
| 8 | **Teacher reality** | Could a teacher run this with the prep time and training they actually have? | Requires setup or expertise that isn't available |
| 9 | **Evidence lane** | Are claims about effect supported by the evidence pack? | "Improves," "boosts," "increases engagement" without a vetted source |

A moment can fail a lens and still be worth saving. The reviewer's job is to say what would fix it.
