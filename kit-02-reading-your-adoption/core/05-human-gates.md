# Human gates

## Gate 1 — Prepare the data
**Owner:** whoever holds the export.
Emails replaced with IDs and the key kept offline? Conversation content removed? Groups under `{{MIN_CELL}}` merged? `{{ELIGIBLE_N}}` from the roster? Summary table computed from these rows in a spreadsheet or deterministic application code, and shown for review?

## Gate 2 — Does it match the buildings?
**Owner:** the person running the read, plus one building leader.
Data describes patterns; people in the buildings know conditions. Does the concentration match who you'd expect? Do you know of a reason for stalls — a login problem, a bad first session, an unclear policy? Record what you know as context, labeled as observation, not data.

## Gate 3 — Choose what to act on
**Owner:** `{{DECISION_OWNER}}`.
Review the audit. Pick responses. Decide how you'll know if they worked — ideally the same export next period, which is the only way this read becomes a trend.

One note on the power users: inviting them to share is a good instinct, but the invitation should come from a person, be voluntary, and never be based on "the data says you're a top user."
