# Variables

| Variable | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `{{DISTRICT}}` | Your district | Lakeview Public Schools |
| `{{TOOL}}` | The AI tool the export comes from | District AI assistant |
| `{{PERIOD}}` | Time span covered | Fall semester (18 weeks) |
| `{{ELIGIBLE_N}}` | Staff who had access, from the roster | 70 |
| `{{STALLED_DEF}}` | Your definition of tried-and-stalled, set before looking | 3 or fewer conversations and active in 2 or fewer weeks |
| `{{MIN_CELL}}` | Smallest group that may be reported | 5 |
| `{{PURPOSE}}` | What the read will inform | Spring professional learning plan |
| `{{DECISION_OWNER}}` | Who acts on it | Director of Teaching and Learning |
| `{{PL_CONSTRAINTS}}` | Time and structures available for learning | Two 45-minute PLC slots per month; one district PD day |

Set `{{STALLED_DEF}}` and `{{MIN_CELL}}` before you look at the data, so the definitions can't bend to fit a story.
